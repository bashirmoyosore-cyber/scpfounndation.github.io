WHO WE ARE

About Our Institute

The National Institute for Scientific Research (NISR) is an independent research institution committed to expanding the boundaries of scientific understanding.

Through interdisciplinary research, advanced laboratory facilities and international partnerships, we strive to address some of the most complex scientific questions of our time.

Our mission is to promote knowledge, encourage innovation and contribute to a safer and more informed world.
