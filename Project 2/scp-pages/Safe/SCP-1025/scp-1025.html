The Encyclopedia of Common Diseases

Item #: SCP-1025

CLASSIFIED
ITEM NUMBERSCP-1025
OBJECT CLASSSAFE
CLEARANCELEVEL 2

SPECIAL CONTAINMENT PROCEDURES

SCP-1025 is to be kept in a locked cabinet at Site-██. Access requires Level 2 clearance or higher. Anyone who reads from SCP-1025 must be quarantined and medically observed until all symptoms have cleared.

Any experiment involving SCP-1025 needs approval from the site director and a medical officer present.

DESCRIPTION

SCP-1025 is a hardcover book of about 1,500 pages. The cover and spine read The Encyclopedia of Common Diseases. The publisher's page says it was printed in 19██ by █████ Press. No other copy has been found, and no record of the publisher exists.

Readers develop the symptoms of any disease they read about, between █ and ██ hours after reading. The symptoms are real, but no pathogen has been found in affected subjects.
